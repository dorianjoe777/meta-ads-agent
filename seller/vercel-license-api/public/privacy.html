Admira IA

Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 6, 2026

Admira IA helps business owners and marketers create, manage, and understand Meta advertising campaigns. This Privacy Policy explains what information we process when you use Admira IA, our website, buyer portal, license services, and Meta integration.

Information we process

Depending on how you use Admira IA, we may process:

  • Account and contact details such as your name, email address, purchase email, license/access key, and support messages.
  • Business setup information you choose to provide, such as business name, offer, website, audience, brand preferences, and creative assets.
  • Meta connection data needed to operate the service, such as Page IDs, ad account IDs, campaign/ad/ad set IDs, pixel or dataset IDs, creative IDs, post IDs, lead form IDs, and related permissions granted by you through Meta.
  • Advertising and performance data available through your authorized Meta account, such as campaign structure, spend, impressions, clicks, conversions, leads, messages, and other reporting metrics.
  • Operational data such as device/install identifiers, dashboard access state, cloud installation status, logs, diagnostics, and security events.

How we use information

We use this information to:

  • Provide and secure Admira IA, including license validation, onboarding, updates, and support.
  • Create, prepare, review, optimize, pause, or report on Meta advertising campaigns when you authorize those actions.
  • Generate business summaries, daily briefs, recommendations, creative drafts, and campaign diagnostics.
  • Improve reliability, prevent abuse, troubleshoot errors, and maintain product safety.

AI and third-party services

Admira IA may connect to AI model providers or infrastructure providers selected or configured by the user. When enabled, relevant prompts, business context, campaign summaries, creative files, or diagnostic data may be processed by those providers only to provide the requested product functionality. We do not sell personal data.

Meta data

Admira IA uses Meta permissions only for the purposes authorized by the user, such as reading ad accounts and pages, creating or managing ads, reading advertising insights, handling lead-related workflows, or preparing creatives. We do not use Meta data for unrelated advertising, resale, or unauthorized profiling.

Data storage and retention

Some product data may be stored locally in the buyer's own installation. License, purchase, support, and cloud access records may be stored in our hosted systems as needed to operate the service. We keep information only for as long as needed for the purposes described above, unless a longer period is required by law, security, billing, dispute resolution, or legitimate operational needs.

Sharing

We may share information with service providers that help us operate the product, such as hosting, email delivery, cloud infrastructure, analytics, support, AI model providers selected for the product workflow, and Meta APIs. We may also disclose information if required by law or to protect users, the service, or our rights.

Your choices and deletion requests

You can revoke Meta permissions from your Meta/Facebook settings at any time. You can also request access, correction, or deletion of personal data by contacting us. For data deletion instructions, visit /data-deletion.

Contact

For privacy questions or deletion requests, contact us at support@admiroia.uboost.lat.

This page is intended to describe Admira IA's privacy practices for product, license, and Meta integration use. It is not a substitute for legal advice for your own business.
