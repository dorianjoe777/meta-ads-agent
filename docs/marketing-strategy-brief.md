# Marketing Strategy Brief: Self-Hosted Meta Ads Operator

## Purpose

This document is the handoff brief for marketing strategy, launch planning, and sales automation work around this product.

The product is a self-hosted Meta Ads agent for beginner marketers, small businesses, info-product sellers, and agencies that want a practical operator in their own PC or VPS instead of another black-box SaaS.

Primary launch market: Spanish-speaking Latin America.

## Product In One Sentence

Un manager de Meta Ads instalado en tu propia computadora o VPS: lee tus resultados, te explica que esta pasando, prepara acciones y solo toca cambios importantes cuando tu lo apruebas.

English version:

A self-hosted Meta Ads manager that runs on your own PC or VPS, reads your campaign data, explains what is happening, prepares actions, and waits for approval before risky changes.

## Core Philosophy

The product should not be sold as a magic button.

It should be sold as a calm, practical ads operator:

- It helps beginners understand what is happening.
- It gives daily structure to ad account management.
- It reduces emotional decisions.
- It catches obvious waste.
- It prepares work that a human can approve.
- It lets the buyer keep control of credentials, account access, and final decisions.

The dashboard exists for visibility, but the chat is the main experience. The buyer should feel like they are talking to a capable media buyer or business manager:

- "Que hacemos hoy?"
- "Revisa si algo esta gastando mal."
- "Prepara una campaña para este producto."
- "Explicame esto como principiante."
- "Que falta antes de activar piloto automatico?"

The product wins when it feels less like software and more like a helpful operator living inside the buyer's own machine.

## Positioning Decision

Sell it as a standalone, self-hosted Meta Ads operator inspired by OpenClaw-style agents.

Do not sell it as an official OpenClaw agent.

Why:

- Beginner marketers do not want to understand OpenClaw.
- "OpenClaw agent" sounds more technical and less finished.
- It creates unnecessary security questions.
- The stronger trust angle is local/VPS ownership, approvals, and buyer-controlled Meta access.

Recommended wording:

> Inspirado en agentes tipo OpenClaw, reconstruido como una herramienta lista para instalar para marketers y negocios que quieren automatizacion sin entregar el control de su cuenta publicitaria.

Avoid:

- "Official OpenClaw agent"
- "Fully autonomous Meta Ads bot"
- "Guaranteed ROAS"
- "No human needed"
- "Official Meta partner tool"

## Buyer Promise

The promise is not guaranteed performance.

The promise is:

- Less confusion.
- Faster daily decisions.
- Better discipline.
- Safer automation.
- Clear approval before risky actions.
- A practical manager that explains things in beginner-friendly language.

Primary buyer-facing promise:

> Deja de abrir Meta Ads a ciegas. Instala un operador que revisa tu cuenta, te explica que importa, prepara acciones y te pide permiso antes de tocar dinero real.

## Ideal Buyer

Best initial buyers:

- Info-product sellers running Meta Ads.
- Coaches, course creators, ecommerce owners, local service businesses.
- Small agencies managing several small accounts.
- Marketers in Latin America who cannot justify a full-time media buyer.
- Business owners who already spend on Meta but do not know what to check every day.

Not ideal for v1:

- Enterprise media teams.
- Buyers expecting a one-click SaaS.
- People with no willingness to follow guided setup.
- Advertisers looking for guaranteed results.
- Accounts with complex catalog, offline events, or advanced tracking needs unless setup is assisted.

## Market Advantage In Latin America

The product can win in LATAM because many buyers have three pains at once:

- They run ads but do not understand the dashboard.
- Agencies are expensive or hard to trust.
- Most automation tools are in English, SaaS-based, and not beginner-friendly.

The product should lean into:

- Spanish-native explanations, not translated-English copy.
- Beginner-friendly onboarding.
- Local ownership of credentials.
- Guided setup call as part of the offer.
- Practical "manager" language instead of technical automation language.

## Trust And Security Message

Security should be explained in friendly language.

Say:

- Corre en tu computadora o VPS.
- Tus claves quedan en tu instalacion.
- Los cambios reales empiezan apagados.
- Las acciones importantes pasan por aprobacion.
- El chat no aprueba por texto libre; por Telegram solo ejecuta aprobaciones exactas cuando el comprador toca el boton correspondiente.
- Puedes empezar con supervision antes de activar piloto automatico.

Avoid overtechnical phrasing in sales pages:

- `.env`
- token
- daemon
- runtime
- Graph API unless the user is in setup material
- dry-run as the main buyer term

Preferred Spanish terms:

- "contraseña del dashboard"
- "modo con supervision"
- "piloto automatico"
- "reglas de aprobacion"
- "cambios reales"
- "acciones preparadas"
- "cola de aprobaciones"

## Pricing Philosophy

Because this is not SaaS at launch, price it like an info-product plus software bundle, not like a cheap template.

The buyer gets:

- The product files.
- The installation process.
- The setup guidance.
- The operating philosophy.
- The agent workflows.
- A practical system for managing ads.

Suggested positioning:

- Not "software license only."
- Not "course only."
- "Sistema instalable + setup guiado."

Potential price bands:

- Low-ticket beta: accessible, limited support.
- Core v1: higher price with setup call.
- Premium: includes installation done-with-you and ad account review.

### Free Codex Automation Guide

This can be a strong list-building offer before selling the Meta Ads Operator.

Offer:

> Recibe gratis una guia simple para crear tu primer mini-agente de automatizacion con OpenAI Codex.

Purpose:

- Attract people already curious about AI agents.
- Teach the basic idea of local automations.
- Build trust before selling the paid ads-specific operator.
- Separate DIY-curious leads from buyers who want a ready system.

The free guide should not give away the full Meta Ads product. It should teach a small, harmless automation, such as:

- Summarizing a daily notes file.
- Watching a folder and creating a simple report.
- Generating a checklist from a text file.
- Drafting a daily business summary from exported CSV data.
- Creating a simple "assistant workflow" that reads one file and writes one recommendation.

The guide should position Codex as the learning doorway:

> Primero aprende como se ve un agente pequeno. Luego, si quieres un operador listo para Meta Ads, instalas el sistema completo.

Recommended guide title options:

- "Tu primer mini-agente con OpenAI Codex"
- "Como crear una automatizacion simple con Codex en una tarde"
- "De cero a tu primer agente local"
- "Guia gratis: crea un mini-agente para tu negocio"

Recommended opt-in promise:

> Te mando por email una guia paso a paso para crear un agente pequeno con Codex, sin venderte humo y sin prometer magia. Es para que entiendas como piensa una automatizacion antes de usar una para Meta Ads.

Bridge to paid product:

- Free guide: "Build a tiny general automation."
- Paid product: "Install a complete Meta Ads operator with setup, dashboard, chat, approvals, and campaign workflows."

Important boundary:

Do not market the free guide as "build this full Meta Ads tool yourself." Market it as a simple first automation so they understand the concept and trust the philosophy.

## Objection Handling

### "No soy tecnico."

Response:

Por eso se vende con setup guiado. El producto esta pensado para que despues de instalarlo lo uses hablando con el agente, no editando archivos.

### "Me da miedo conectar Meta."

Response:

Usas tu propia app/token de Meta. La conexion queda bajo tu control, en tu PC o VPS. No estas entregando tu cuenta a una plataforma SaaS desconocida.

### "Puede gastar dinero solo?"

Response:

Por defecto no. Primero trabaja con supervision. Los cambios importantes quedan en aprobacion. El piloto automatico es opcional y se configura con reglas.

### "Esto garantiza mejores resultados?"

Response:

No. Ayuda a operar con mas orden, detectar riesgos, preparar acciones y evitar errores comunes. El resultado depende de oferta, creativo, tracking, cuenta y mercado.

### "Por que no usar una agencia?"

Response:

Una agencia puede ser util, pero este producto cubre el trabajo diario repetitivo y da visibilidad. Para muchos negocios pequenos es un primer operador antes de contratar equipo.

## Claims To Avoid

- Guaranteed lower CPA.
- Fully autonomous profit machine.
- Official Meta approval.
- Official OpenClaw product.
- No setup required.

Safer claims:

- Helps explain.
- Helps prepare actions.
- Helps reduce missed daily checks.
- Helps create approval-based workflows.
- Helps beginners understand ads with simpler language.
